Try the 21 days

This runs the app's own mission rules. Look after Miso for a few weeks and watch what each day does. Nothing is saved.

Rescue: wellness starts at 10 and reaches 100 on the 21st goal-met day in a row. On day 8 it is at 45, with the coat, the energy and the noise fear healed.

Raise: wellness starts at 70 and reaches 100 on day 21.

A slip before day 21 starts the count at 0 again; the best run is kept. Turn on JavaScript to try it.

21 days, then 21 more

The journey doesn't end at recovery, and one bad day never wipes it out.

  1. Recovery

    Days 1 to 21

    Goal-met days in a row. A slip before day 21 starts the count over, but your best run is kept and every day stays in your record.

  2. Consistency

    Days 22 to 42

    21 more goal-met days make it last, with rewards at one, two and three weeks. From here a slip gives your dog an issue to clear, never a restart.

  3. Thriving

    After day 42

    A reward every 30 well days. An issue from a slip clears with 3, 7 or 14 goal-met days in a row, depending on how often slips came.

What PAWSE is, and isn't

A self-awareness and habit-change tool. Not medical treatment, therapy or a cure, and your dog's conditions are illustrative. 21 days is PAWSE's mission length, not a medical recovery time. If a habit is hurting you, talk to a doctor or someone you trust. It never tracks calories, weight or food restriction.

Questions

What is PAWSE?

An Android app for changing a habit you choose, with a 3D companion at its centre. You check in each day; goal-met days help your companion heal or learn, and slips have a cost it shows. It also has urge support, a calendar, insights and a weekly review.

How does Rescue differ from Raise?

Both are the same 21-day mission. A rescued dog starts neglected, at 10 wellness, and heals six conditions. A raised dog starts healthy, at 70, and learns six skills. You choose once, when you adopt.

What happens if I slip?

You log it in a tap, and the app tells you what still stands and what puts it right. In the first 21 days a slip starts the count over, keeping your best run. After that it gives your dog an issue that clears with 3, 7 or 14 goal-met days in a row.

Does it work for different habits?

Yes: food orders, shopping, scrolling, smoking, vaping, drinking, or anything you name. Each habit gets its own companion and its own records, so you can work on more than one without them mixing.

Is PAWSE medical treatment?

No. It's a self-awareness tool, not treatment, therapy or medical advice. If a habit is harming you, please talk to a doctor or a professional.

Is my data public?

No. Your records are saved privately to your account and protected so only you can read them. There are no public profiles, ads or analytics, and photos never leave your phone.
