Your habit, with a face

Pawse is a companion app for Android that shows you how close you are to your goal, one small daily check-in at a time. Pick something you want to do less, adopt a 3D dog, and check in each day. Good days help it heal. A slip is information, not a verdict.

Late-night food orders, impulse buys, doomscrolling, smoking or vaping, drinking, or a habit you name yourself. You set the goal: stop, cut down, or understand it first.

How Pawse works

Choose a goal, check in once a day, and your companion responds. These are the parts.

Rescue it or raise it

When you adopt, you choose where your dog starts. Both take 21 goal-met days in a row; the difference is what those days do.

Rescue Harder

Starts neglected, at 10 wellness. Heals six conditions.

  • 1 Itchy, patchy coat
  • 3 Low energy
  • 6 Startles at noises
  • 10 Doesn't know how to play
  • 15 Afraid of being left alone
  • 21 Afraid of people

Raise Easier

Starts healthy, at 70 wellness. Learns six skills.

  • 1 Learns its name
  • 3 Settles into a routine
  • 6 Plays fetch
  • 10 Explores with confidence
  • 15 Stays calm alone
  • 21 Grows strong

The numbers are goal-met days in a row. These describe your dog, not you.

Check in in seconds

Goal met, a slip, or an urge you got through. One tap, and your pet responds.

Get through an urge

Pause, name it, and pick a way through: breathe, move, add friction, reach out.

Watch the days add up

A calendar of your days, your runs and milestones. Forgot yesterday? Fill it in.

See your patterns

See how close you are to your goal: this week against last, and slips per week over two months.

One pet per habit

Each habit gets its own companion and records. Nothing on one changes another.

See how close you are to your goal

Set a goal: stop, cut down to a number a week, or understand it first. Pawse holds it up against your daily check-ins, so you see where you stand, not only how many days you've counted.

  • Where you are against your goal: on target, closing in, or still building

Try the 21 days

This runs the app's own mission rules. Look after Miso for a few weeks and watch what each day does. Nothing is saved.

Rescue: wellness starts at 10 and reaches 100 on the 21st goal-met day in a row. On day 8 it is at 45, with the coat, the energy and the noise fear healed.

Raise: wellness starts at 70 and reaches 100 on day 21.

A slip before day 21 starts the count at 0 again; the best run is kept. Turn on JavaScript to try it.

21 days, then 21 more

The journey doesn't end at recovery, and one bad day never wipes it out.

  1. Recovery

    Days 1 to 21

    Goal-met days in a row. A slip before day 21 starts the count over, but your best run is kept and every day stays in your record.

  2. Consistency

    Days 22 to 42

    21 more goal-met days make it last, with rewards at one, two and three weeks. From here a slip gives your dog an issue to clear, never a restart.

  3. Thriving

    After day 42

    A reward every 30 well days. An issue from a slip clears with 3, 7 or 14 goal-met days in a row, depending on how often slips came.

Privacy and honesty

What happens to your records, said plainly.

Your records

  • No ads, and we never sell your data
  • When you're signed in, your pets, slips, goal-met days, urges and notes are saved to your own account and are private to it
  • Photos never leave your phone
  • An optional PIN encrypts what's stored on the phone
  • Export or delete everything, any time
  • No calorie, weight or food-restriction tracking

Where AI comes in

When you log a slip while signed in, Pawse sends the pet's name, the kind of habit, the amount, the answers you picked and any short note you typed to an AI model, through our server, so it can write a short note back. For the weekly summary it sends last week's totals.

The AI service is asked not to keep that text, but we can't check what it does on its side. We save only the reply, so the same slip isn't sent twice. We do not sell data.

To learn whether Pawse helps people keep going, I may look at simple counts from the records, such as how many days people check in. No advertising or analytics trackers run in the app or on this page.

What Pawse is, and isn't

A self-awareness and habit tool. Not medical treatment, therapy or a cure, and your dog's conditions are illustrative. 21 days is Pawse's mission length, not a medical recovery time.

If a habit is harming you, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust. If you are in danger or in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country.

Questions

What is Pawse?

An Android app for changing a habit you choose, with a 3D companion at its centre. You check in each day and Pawse shows how close you are to your goal. Goal-met days help your companion heal or learn, and slips have a cost it shows. It also has urge support, a calendar, insights and a weekly review.

How does Rescue differ from Raise?

Both are the same 21-day mission. A rescued dog starts neglected, at 10 wellness, and heals six conditions. A raised dog starts healthy, at 70, and learns six skills. You choose once, when you adopt.

What happens if I slip?

You log it in a tap, and the app tells you what still stands and what puts it right. A slip is information, not a verdict. In the first 21 days it starts the count over, keeping your best run. After that it gives your dog an issue that clears with 3, 7 or 14 goal-met days in a row.

Does it work for different habits?

Yes: food orders, shopping, scrolling, smoking, vaping, drinking, or anything you name. Each habit gets its own companion and its own records.

Is Pawse medical treatment?

No. It's a self-awareness and habit tool, not treatment, therapy or medical advice. If a habit is harming you, please talk to a doctor or a professional.

Is my data public?

No. Your records are saved privately to your account, and there are no public profiles or ads. We never sell your data. Slip details are sent to an AI model to write a short note; see "Privacy and honesty" above for exactly what.
