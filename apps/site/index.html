Numbers are easy to ignore

A streak counter gives you a number. It doesn't look up when you come home. PAWSE puts a companion where the number was, so your habit has a face.

Who it's for. Anyone who wants to change something they keep doing: late-night food orders, impulse buys, doomscrolling, smoking or vaping, drinking, or a habit you name yourself. You set the goal: stop, cut down, or understand it first.

What it is. An Android app with a 3D companion at its centre. Each day you record a goal-met day, a slip, or an urge you got through. Your companion's wellness, mood and missions follow those records and nothing else.

How it treats slips. A slip is information, not a verdict. Your companion feels it, your record keeps every good day, and the app shows exactly what puts it right. You pick how it talks to you: Gentle, Honest or Direct.

What it isn't. Not therapy, not medical treatment and not a promise. It's a self-awareness tool that makes your days visible. It never tracks calories, weight or food restriction.

Rescue it or raise it

When you adopt, you choose where your dog starts. Both take 21 goal-met days in a row; the difference is what those days do.

Try the 21 days

This runs the app's own mission rules. Look after Miso for a few weeks and watch what each day does.

Rescue: wellness starts at 10 and reaches 100 on the 21st goal-met day in a row. On day 8 it is at 45, with the coat, the energy and the noise fear healed.

Raise: wellness starts at 70 and reaches 100 on day 21, learning a skill on days 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 and 21.

A slip before day 21 starts the count at 0 again; the best run is kept. Turn on JavaScript to try it.

21 days, then 21 more

  1. Recovery

    Goal-met days in a row. Six conditions heal, or six skills are learned, on days 1, 3, 6, 10, 15 and 21. Days with nothing recorded, rest days and paused days count neither way.

  2. A slip starts it over

    Before day 21, a slip sets the count back to 0. It's said plainly, with what it takes to put right. Your best run is kept, and every day you logged stays in your record.

  3. Then it lasts

    Once recovered, 21 more goal-met days build consistency, with rewards at one, two and three weeks. Then your dog is thriving, with a reward every 30 well days. From here a slip gives it an issue to clear in 3, 7 or 14 days in a row, never a restart.

21 days is PAWSE's mission length, not a medical recovery time.

What PAWSE is, and isn't

PAWSE is a self-awareness and habit-change tool. It is not medical treatment, therapy or a cure, and your dog's conditions are illustrative. If a habit is hurting you, talk to a doctor or someone you trust; the app shows crisis support when what you type needs it. Stopping alcohol suddenly can be dangerous for some people, so talk to a doctor first.
