# Validation experiments (thresholds fixed before running)

## 1. exp-problem-interviews (designed)

**Hypothesis:** Heads of sales at 3-10 seller B2B teams on HubSpot/Pipedrive can name a deal lost or delayed in the last 90 days because an email follow-up or CRM update was missed, and spend >= 2 h/week per rep on CRM upkeep.

- **Participants:** 15 heads of sales / sales-led founders, 3-10 sellers, B2B, on HubSpot or Pipedrive.
- **Recruitment:** Personal network, LinkedIn search by title + company size, r/sales and founder communities (no paid panels without approval).
- **Procedure:** 30-min problem interview using the last real example (no pitching); screen for CRM, mail provider, who is admin, current spend.
- **Cost:** Time only (~12 h).
- **Duration:** 10 days
- **Success threshold:** >= 8 of 15 give a specific recent example with an estimated deal value AND report >= 2 h/week/rep upkeep.
- **Failure threshold:** <= 4 of 15 can name a concrete example, or most say a routine already solves it.
- **Confounders:** Interviewer leading; recall bias; network skew towards tech companies.
- **Decision:** Pass -> run exp-concierge-audit. Fail -> STOP this wedge; re-test w-followup-accountability with consultants only, or stop.

## 2. exp-concierge-audit (designed)

**Hypothesis:** A manually produced 'stale pipeline audit' (deals with unanswered customer email, overdue promised next steps, threads attached to wrong deals) is valuable enough that teams pay for it.

- **Participants:** Interviewees who passed exp-problem-interviews.
- **Recruitment:** Offer at end of interview.
- **Procedure:** With written consent, the head of sales exports CRM deals and shares (or screen-shares) relevant threads; analyst produces the audit by hand within 48 h; offer a monthly repeat for a fee.
- **Cost:** Time; no API access needed.
- **Duration:** 3 weeks
- **Success threshold:** >= 5 teams accept the free audit AND >= 3 pay >= $100 for a second month (actual payment, not intent).
- **Failure threshold:** < 3 accept the audit, or 0 pay.
- **Confounders:** Data sharing reluctance may reduce uptake independently of value; novelty effect.
- **Decision:** Pass -> build exp-paid-pilot with provider-approved integrations. Fail -> STOP.

## 3. exp-permission-test (designed)

**Hypothesis:** Target buyers will connect their mailboxes and CRM to a new vendor's app.

- **Participants:** Concierge-audit participants plus 10 cold prospects from the same segment.
- **Recruitment:** As above.
- **Procedure:** Show the exact Google/Microsoft consent screens (mocked from the documented scopes) and ask them to complete a test connection to an unverified test app within the 100-user cap.
- **Cost:** Small; test app registration is free.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks (parallel)
- **Success threshold:** >= 60% complete the connection; where an M365 admin is required, the buyer is the admin in >= 70% of cases.
- **Failure threshold:** < 40% complete, or IT approval needed in most tenants.
- **Confounders:** Unverified-app warning screen inflates refusals relative to a verified app.
- **Decision:** Fail -> pursue the CRM-API-only variant (unknown crm-logged-email-sufficient) or STOP.

## 4. exp-native-benchmark (designed)

**Hypothesis:** Native CRM features (HubSpot, Pipedrive) leave >= 30% of deal updates or thread associations wrong or missing on real data.

- **Participants:** 2-3 audit participants who consent.
- **Recruitment:** From exp-concierge-audit.
- **Procedure:** Compare the manual audit with what the CRM's native AI/inbox sync recorded for the same deals.
- **Cost:** Time.
- **Duration:** 1 week
- **Success threshold:** >= 30% of updates missing or wrong in native tooling.
- **Failure threshold:** < 10% (incumbent already solves it).
- **Confounders:** Teams may not have enabled native features.
- **Decision:** Fail -> STOP (incumbent bundling).

## 5. exp-mockup (designed)

**Hypothesis:** A 'needs attention today' approval queue is the interface managers would use weekly.

- **Participants:** Same participants.
- **Recruitment:** -
- **Procedure:** Clickable mockup populated with their own audit results; observe a weekly pipeline review.
- **Cost:** ~2 days design.
- **Duration:** 1 week
- **Success threshold:** >= 4 of 6 use it in their actual pipeline review.
- **Failure threshold:** They prefer the spreadsheet/PDF audit.
- **Confounders:** Politeness.
- **Decision:** Fail -> keep it a service, not software.

## 6. exp-provider-clarification (designed)

**Hypothesis:** Google will confirm in writing that a CRM-companion app that does not send mail fits the approved Gmail restricted-scope use cases; CASA cost is affordable (< $5k/yr).

- **Participants:** Google OAuth verification support; one CASA-authorised lab.
- **Recruitment:** -
- **Procedure:** Ask in writing (requires approval before contacting third parties).
- **Cost:** Free to ask; CASA fee unknown.
- **Duration:** 2-4 weeks
- **Success threshold:** Written confirmation and a quote < $5k/yr.
- **Failure threshold:** Rejection, or a quote > $15k/yr before revenue.
- **Confounders:** Answers may be non-committal.
- **Decision:** Fail -> Microsoft-first or CRM-API-only variant.

## 7. exp-paid-pilot (designed)

**Hypothesis:** Teams keep paying once software replaces the concierge.

- **Participants:** Paying concierge customers.
- **Recruitment:** -
- **Procedure:** Ship the minimal CRM companion within the 100-user unverified cap / a single M365 tenant admin consent; 60-day paid pilot.
- **Cost:** Build ~6-10 weeks.
- **Duration:** 60 days
- **Success threshold:** >= 3 teams pay for month 3 and approve >= 50% of proposed updates weekly.
- **Failure threshold:** < 2 retain.
- **Confounders:** Founder hand-holding inflates retention.
- **Decision:** Pass -> PROCEED to CASA verification. Fail -> STOP.
